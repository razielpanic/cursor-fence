Cursor Fence: icon samples

Four directions on the macOS 26 rounded-square grid (824 pt shape on a 1024 canvas). Each is shown large, at Dock and Finder sizes, and in a row with the others.

A · The wall

A display split down the middle: the hatched half is off-limits, the lit half is yours, and the pointer is stopped by a glowing seam. Reads as "displays" and "boundary" at once.

B · Glass pane

A big pointer meets a pane of glass with a flash and motion lines, matching the app's real rebound and glow. Leans into the Liquid Glass look.

C · Picket fence

The name, literally: a white picket fence with the pointer kept on the far side. The friendliest and easiest to spot in the Dock.

D · Rebound

A utility-style graphite icon: a dimmed zone, a light beam, and the pointer's path bouncing off it. Sits with Apple's own system utilities.
